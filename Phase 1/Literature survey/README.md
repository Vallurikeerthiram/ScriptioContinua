# Literature Survey

This folder contains research papers, reports, and presentation materials related to scriptio continua and word segmentation.

## Key Topics

- **Word Segmentation**: Papers on automatic word segmentation in various languages (Thai, Greek, Latin, Nguni).
- **Scriptio Continua**: Research on processing and digitizing text without spaces.
- **Morphological Analysis**: Toolkits and models for morphological segmentation.
- **Large Language Models**: Recent research on using LLMs for ancient script restoration.

## Contents

- Academic PDFs and survey papers.
- Project reports and presentation points (`ppt_points.txt`).
